Voice Loop virtual audio setup

Windows audio paths use VB-CABLE and Hi-Fi Cable & ASIO Bridge by VB-Audio Software.
Origin: https://www.vb-cable.com/ and https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
These are donationware. All contributions are welcome. Donate/pay for your license:
https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

The original, unmodified VB-CABLE package is included. Hi-Fi Cable is downloaded
directly from VB-Audio during audio setup. Voice Loop does not modify the signed
drivers. VoiceLoop Mic and VoiceLoop Speaker are endpoint display names.
VB-Audio's own license applies to its software; it is not covered by Voice Loop's MIT license.
Professional and organizational use is subject to VB-Audio's licensing terms.

macOS uses unmodified BlackHole 2ch and 16ch 0.7.1 by Existential Audio, GPL-3.0.
Source and license: https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole/tree/v0.7.1
Public Core Audio aggregate devices provide the VoiceLoop names.

Uninstalling Voice Loop preserves recordings and shared audio drivers. Remove
shared drivers only with their vendor's instructions when other apps no longer need them.
